Project Goal

This website, **Al Karam Revenue Documents**, has been specially developed to manage and securely store all agricultural land records. All rights of this website belong exclusively to its owner, **Mr. Shahid Ikram Malik**.

Brief Introduction

Mr. Shahid Ikram Malik is a trustworthy, hardworking, and visionary individual who is introducing a modern and innovative system for the secure, organized, and digital management of agricultural land records. He has extensive experience in land management, agricultural documentation, record protection, and digital security. Under his leadership, the Al Karam Revenue Documents website has been created to ensure that every type of agricultural record remains protected, transparent, and easily accessible in one secure place.
