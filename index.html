Shahid Ikram Malik - Profile

My name is **Shahid Ikram Malik**. I am an advocate by profession, dedicated to justice, public service, and the protection of community rights. Along with my legal career, I am a landlord, politician, and a proud representative of the **Pakistan People’s Party (PPP)**. I currently serve as the **UC Chairman**, where I work with commitment for development, welfare, and strengthening grassroots governance.

I am also a businessman with diverse ventures across multiple sectors. I run an **APC medicine factory**, supply fish, trade in poultry, and operate as a feeds supplier. I am the owner of **Al Karam Khairpur Feeds Mill**, located in the KSEZ Zone, Khairpur, which is known for quality, reliability, and modern feed production.

With integrity, leadership, and dedication, I aim to create opportunities, support local farmers, and contribute to economic growth while continuing to serve my people with passion and responsibility.
